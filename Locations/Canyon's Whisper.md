Canyon's Whisper is a small/medium village located in a large clearing of a forest. While many trails exist in and out of the village, its main throughway joins with the King's road about mile away. This intersection is naturally hidden and only the most observant travelers would notice it when passing through. Those that do and detour into town are tempted by its friendly people and the safety the forest provides.

Its population consists of humans, elves, dwarves, and a handful of halfings, and most people of Canyon's Whisper are tolerant of other races. 

It's history is [here](../History/History.md#The%20Founding%20of%20Canyon's%20Whisper).
# Services
While the town is large enough to provide for its residents, it can greatly benefit from investments into its shops, inns, temples, services and farms. The below section details the levels of each service and the benefits they bring.

See [Improving Canyon's Whisper](../House%20Rules/Improving%20Canyon's%20Whisper.md) for rules and requirements of each service.
## The Town
*NOTE: No other service can be raised higher in level than the town itself.*

**Level**: 0

**Offerings**: None.
## Market Services
**Level**: 0

**Offerings**: Common equipment useful to a small farm community only. For example: rope, lanterns, iron spikes are available but not wolfsbane, spyglasses, or thieves tools. Questionable items like backpacks, rations, and the like have a 35% chance of being available (re-roll weekly).
## Inn Services
**Level**: 0

**Offerings**: One inn/tavern called The Braying Ass. The food is edible but not enjoyable, and the ale is only slightly better. The rooms are not very clean. You'll get a full nights rest and a bug bite or two.
# Military Services
**Level**: 0

**Offerings**: None. There's no guard, only the [Mayor Philtrum](../Notable%20NPCs/Canyons%20Whisper/Mayor%20Philtrum.md) and a couple of trusted residents that respond to problems. The town is fucked if raided by any barely organized force.
## Temple Services
**Level**: 0

**Offerings**: None. The shack that acts as the town's place of worship is kept standing by an old man in his spare time. If you can find him, he'll provide hugs and advice about keeping his goat happy but no healing.
## Magical Services
**Level**: 0

**Offerings**: None. This place is so devoid of magic that even "Got Your Nose" fails to cast properly.
